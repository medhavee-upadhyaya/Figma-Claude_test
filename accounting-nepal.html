Nepal Tax Guide 2082/83

Key Tax Rates & Compliance
Requirements for FY 2025–26

Nepal's self-assessment tax system governed by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). Understanding these rates helps you stay compliant and plan effectively.

1%–36%
Personal Income Tax
Progressive rates with NPR 5,00,000 basic exemption (single) / NPR 6,00,000 (married)
25%
Corporate Tax (Standard)
Standard CIT rate; 20% for manufacturing; 5–15% concessions for priority sectors
13%
VAT (Standard Rate)
Mandatory registration threshold: NPR 50 lakh annual turnover. 0% for exports.
15%
Non-Resident WHT
On Nepal-sourced service fees, royalties, interest, and commission payments to non-residents
Income Tax Return Deadline
Within 3 months after fiscal year end — by mid-October (Kartik). Late filing incurs 0.1% per day penalty.
Advance Tax (4 Installments)
Required if annual tax liability exceeds NPR 40,000. Payable in Poush, Chaitra, Ashad & Aswin.
Audit Report Submission
Companies must submit audited financials within 6 months of fiscal year end (by mid-January).
VAT Return Filing
Monthly by the 25th of the following month. Quarterly for small taxpayers approved by IRD.
SSF Contribution
Employer: 20% of basic salary. Employee: 11%. Total 31% deposited to Social Security Fund monthly.
Digital Services Tax (DST)
2% on digital services by non-residents to Nepali users. Applies above NPR 30 lakh annual transactions.
